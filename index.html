Xiangyu Wang

Ph.D. Student in Mathematics

I am a Ph.D. student in Mathematics at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, advised by Xiaochun Li. My primary research area is harmonic analysis, particularly Fourier restriction, Kakeya-type problems, decoupling, and local smoothing. I am also interested in additive combinatorics and analytic number theory.

Research Interests

My research focuses on harmonic analysis, including geometric and analytic methods related to restriction estimates and wave equations.

  • Fourier Restriction
  • Kakeya-type Problems
  • Decoupling
  • Local Smoothing
  • Additive Combinatorics
  • Analytic Number Theory
